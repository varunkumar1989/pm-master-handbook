## Hypothetical examples and figures

- **Case studies are hypothetical scenarios.** They describe the *type* of product a company is known for; the problems, evidence, experiments, numbers and outcomes are invented for teaching. They do not describe any company's actual data, plans, systems, decisions or results.
- **Datasets are synthetic.** All CSV files are generated from a fixed random seed and contain no real people, customers or transactions.
- **Wireframes are illustrative.** All screens are simplified sketches made for this handbook. They are not screenshots of any real product.
- **Prices, features and capabilities change.** Statements about tools, cloud services and AI models are general and may be out of date; check the vendor's current documentation.
