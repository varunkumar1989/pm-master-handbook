Independent practice material. All scenarios are hypothetical; company names only identify the type of product. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any company named.

How to use this set

Each scenario is a classic root-cause-analysis (RCA) interview question: a metric changed and you must find out why. Practise out loud with a partner who plays the interviewer. Your partner should use the Solutions PDF, which lists the facts to reveal only when you ask the right question.

What interviewers look for

SkillWhat good looks like
ClarifyingPins down the metric definition, scope and timing before investigating
StructureStates a framework up front and returns to it as hypotheses are ruled out
PrioritisationChecks the most likely and cheapest-to-verify causes first (data, recent releases, segments)
Data thinkingAsks for specific cuts: by platform, version, region, channel, funnel step
ConclusionNames the root cause, an immediate fix, how to measure recovery and how to prevent recurrence
CommunicationSummarises progress regularly; speaks with confident, statement-style delivery

Q1 · Cart additions down 15% in three days

Product

E-commerce marketplace (Flipkart-style)

Metric

Add-to-cart events

Difficulty

Medium

Interviewer prompt

Cart additions on a large e-commerce marketplace have fallen about 15% over the last three days. Diagnose the problem.

Hints (use only if stuck)
  • Define the metric precisely before you investigate it.
  • Which surface is affected: website, iOS app or Android app?
  • What shipped in the last week?

Q2 · Food-delivery orders down 10% in one city

Product

Food delivery (Swiggy/Zomato-style)

Metric

Completed orders

Difficulty

Medium

Interviewer prompt

Completed orders in Bengaluru fell 10% this week versus last week. Other cities are flat. What happened?

Hints (use only if stuck)
  • Is it demand (fewer people ordering) or supply (fewer restaurants or riders)?
